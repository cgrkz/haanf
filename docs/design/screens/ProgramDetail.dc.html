Weeks 1–4

Easy sessions, building from 20 to 35 minutes. Week 4 is lighter.

Weeks 5–8

One interval session a week, building to 4 x 4 minutes.

The research behind it

In a 2007 study, 4 x 4 intervals raised VO2max more than steady running. That study used moderately trained men, so this plan builds up to them more gently.

Stay consistent

If you go 7 days without a program workout, you'll repeat that week. After 30 days, the program restarts from Week 1. AI workouts are always available, but they don't count toward your program.
