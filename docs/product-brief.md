# Haanf product brief

Haanf is an AI-native endurance training app for Android. It is built for the builder and friends, amateurs getting into cardio. Activities are outdoor running, cycling and treadmill.

## Two modes

**Program mode** gives fixed, multi-week, research-backed plans. The AI coaches during a session but never changes the plan.

**AI mode** asks how much time you have and how you feel, reads recent workouts and recovery data, and the AI decides the workout. It can also call for a benchmark. A separate rule check approves each workout.

AI mode stays available during a program, but it avoids hard sessions the day before or after a hard program session.

## Program reset rules

- 7 days without a program workout repeats the last week trained.
- 30 days without one restarts at Week 1.
- AI workouts do not stop this clock.

## Benchmarks

Benchmarks are fixed, research-based tests. The AI chooses when and which, never the protocol.

## Talk test

The user speaks during a workout and the app assesses comfort from the audio. It is optional for normal workouts. Combined with heart rate, it estimates the personal threshold where talking gets hard.

## Shared by both modes

Workout history, benchmarks, vitals and safety rules.

## Devices

The first devices are a Google Pixel Watch 2 and a friend's Amazfit GTR 2e. More wearables come later, so heart rate sits behind a device-independent boundary. Android only, because the Pixel Watch needs it.

## Phase 0

Phase 0 proves the three riskiest pieces on real hardware: live heart rate, talk test recording and Health Connect data. It builds no programs, AI coach or final design. See [phase-0-results.md](phase-0-results.md).
